About Us

About Jiangsu Yuren Group

Jiangsu Yuren Group was founded in 1990, specializing in military and police professional equipment manufacturing. Over 36 years of development, we have become a leading domestic supplier.

Located in Yancheng, Jiangsu Province, our modern production base has an annual capacity exceeding 6 million units. Products include tactical boots, military boots, uniforms, knitwear, and technical fabrics.
